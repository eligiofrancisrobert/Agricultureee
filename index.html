Tanikala is a web-based agricultural platform designed to connect farmers, buyers, and suppliers while organizing agricultural product and user information in one place.

Tanikala supports three registration roles: farmers, buyers, and suppliers. Available features depend on the user's role and account access.

Open the registration page, choose your account type, complete the required information, and submit the required documents when applicable.

Some accounts require administrator review before access is granted. Please wait for your application to be reviewed and check your account status.

Use a strong, unique password and never share your password or login verification codes with others.

Farmers can provide their farm information and select the crops requested during registration. The submitted information is associated with their application.

Buyers can register for a buyer account and provide their buyer information and product interests as requested during registration.

Suppliers can choose the supplier role, enter their business information, identify the products they supply, and submit the required registration documents.

Tanikala is accessed through a web browser, so you can use a compatible phone, tablet, or computer.

Check your email and password, ensure your account is eligible for access, and follow the available password recovery process if you have forgotten your password.

Available editing options depend on the profile features enabled for your account. Contact support if you need help updating your information.

Product-interest updates depend on the profile management features available to your account.

The required documents depend on the fields shown during supplier registration. Make sure the required information and files are complete before submitting.
